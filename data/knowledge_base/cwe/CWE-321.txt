CWE-321: Use of Hard-coded Cryptographic Key

Abstraction: Variant

Likelihood of Exploit: High

Applicable Platforms: Not Language-Specific, ICS/OT

## Description
The product uses a hard-coded, unchangeable cryptographic key.

## Common Consequences
- Access Control: Bypass Protection Mechanism, Gain Privileges or Assume Identity, Read Application Data — If hard-coded cryptographic keys are used, it is almost certain that malicious users will gain access through the account in question.
