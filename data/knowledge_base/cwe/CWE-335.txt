CWE-335: Incorrect Usage of Seeds in Pseudo-Random Number Generator (PRNG)

Abstraction: Base

Applicable Platforms: Not Language-Specific

## Description
The product uses a Pseudo-Random Number Generator (PRNG) but does not correctly manage seeds.

## Extended Description
PRNGs are deterministic and, while their output appears random, they cannot actually create entropy. They rely on cryptographically secure and unique seeds for entropy so proper seeding is critical to the secure operation of the PRNG. Management of seeds could be broken down into two main areas: (1) protecting seeds as cryptographic material (such as a cryptographic key); (2) whenever possible, using a uniquely generated seed from a cryptographically secure source PRNGs require a seed as input to generate a stream of numbers that are functionally indistinguishable from random numbers. While the output is, in many cases, sufficient for cryptographic uses, the output of any PRNG is directly determined by the seed provided as input.
