CWE-1053: Missing Documentation for Design

Abstraction: Base

Applicable Platforms: Not Language-Specific

## Description
The product does not have documentation that represents how it is designed.

## Common Consequences
- Other: Reduce Maintainability, Increase Analytical Complexity — This issue can make it more difficult to understand and maintain the product. It can make it more difficult and time-consuming to detect and/or fix vulnerabilities.
