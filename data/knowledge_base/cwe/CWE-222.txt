CWE-222: Truncation of Security-relevant Information

Abstraction: Base

Applicable Platforms: Not Language-Specific, Not Technology-Specific

## Description
The product truncates the display, recording, or processing of security-relevant information in a way that can obscure the source or nature of an attack.

## Common Consequences
- Non-Repudiation: Hide Activities — The source of an attack will be difficult or impossible to determine. This can allow attacks to the system to continue without notice.
